My name is Qixiu Cheng (程启秀). I am a Professor in the School of Transportation at Southeast University (SEU), and I am a Senior Member of IEEE. Before joining SEU as a Professor in Transportation Engineering, I was a Senior Lecturer in AI and Data Analytics at the University of Bristol. I obtained my Ph.D. in Transportation Engineering from SEU in March 2021. I'm now focusing on exploring smart mobility systems with quantitative methods from multiple disciplines, e.g., transportation engineering, operations research, statistical analysis, and artificial intelligence. I am broadly interested in the research topics on transportation network modeling and optimization, traffic flow theory and control, and AI applications in transportation.
